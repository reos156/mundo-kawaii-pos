# Mundo Kawaii POS MVP — ODD Task Ledger

## Objective
Build a web POS for one physical store and one register, using Supabase and a Bun/Turborepo monorepo with React, TypeScript, Vite, TanStack Router, and Tailwind CSS. The MVP must keep sales, collections, reservations, inventory, and physical cash as distinct concepts.

## Problem
The store needs to manage physical products in whole units, several simultaneous pre-accounts, customer reservations, cash and manually verified bank transfers, inventory movements, cash sessions, consultations, administration, and permissions without counting pending activity as completed sales or cash.

## Why
The POS is a system of record for product availability and store money. Checkout and stock transitions must be atomic, authorized, auditable, and understandable to cashiers and administrators.

## Scope
### Included
- Single store and single register; Spanish (Colombia) user interface and COP amounts represented as whole pesos.
- Product catalog and integer inventory, including stock receipts, authorized adjustments, and movement history.
- Multiple independent, persistent pre-accounts.
- Customer reservations that hold available stock without creating a sale, collection, or cash movement.
- Cash checkout and manual bank-transfer verification.
- Cash sessions, opening float, cash movements, expected-vs-counted closing, and discrepancy visibility.
- Role-based administration and authorization; sales, collections, reservations, stock, and cash consultations.
- Internal non-fiscal receipt suitable for browser printing.
- Supabase Auth/Postgres, row-level security, database-enforced authorization, and transactional database operations for critical transitions.

### Excluded unless the user changes scope
- DIAN electronic invoicing or claims that the internal receipt is a fiscal invoice.
- Multiple stores/registers, offline operation, payment-provider integrations, and automatic bank verification.
- Supplier purchasing workflows, serial/lot tracking, fractional quantities, and dedicated thermal-printer integration.
- Split-tender checkout; the initial workflow accepts one payment method per checkout.
- Production deployment or use of production credentials.

## Constraints and accepted decisions
- Work from the requirements in the current user request only. Preserve pre-existing untracked planning artifacts; do not use them as product requirements or overwrite them.
- The internal receipt is non-fiscal; electronic invoicing is outside this MVP.
- A pre-account is not a sale or payment and does not hold stock.
- A customer reservation is separate from a pre-account and sale; it reduces available stock until converted, cancelled, or expired.
- A transfer awaiting manual verification holds checkout stock but is not a confirmed sale or cash movement. Approval confirms the payment and sale and consumes the held inventory; rejection releases the hold. The user selected this rule.
- Cash checkout confirms the cash payment and sale and records the stock and cash movements atomically.
- Initial roles are cashier and administrator/supervisor; privileged operations are checked server-side and audited.
- Product catalog changes, stock receipts, and stock adjustments are administrator-only; cashiers have read-only inventory access. This permission boundary was explicitly confirmed while resuming POS-04.
- Operator-provisioned users authenticate with email and password; public signup remains disabled. This method was explicitly selected while resuming POS-05.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Domain language explicitly distinguishes pre-accounts, reservations, checkout/payment verification, confirmed sales, inventory movements, and cash sessions.
2. User and system flows are documented in Mermaid, including cash checkout, pending/approved/rejected transfer, reservation, and cash close.
3. Supabase authorization cannot be bypassed by UI-only checks; privileged writes validate roles in the database.
4. Product quantities and stock are whole units, COP prices use integer pesos, and stock cannot become negative under concurrent operations.
5. Multiple pre-accounts can remain open independently; editing or abandoning one does not create sales, payments, inventory movements, or cash movements.
6. Active reservations and pending transfer checkouts reduce available stock without being reported as completed sales.
7. Transfer approval/rejection is authorized and audited; approval creates the sale and inventory movement, while rejection releases the held stock.
8. Confirmed cash checkout records sale, payment, inventory movement, and cash movement atomically; transfers never inflate physical cash.
9. Closing a cash session shows expected cash, counted cash, and any discrepancy without rewriting prior movements.
10. Consultations distinguish completed sales, pending/confirmed/rejected collections, reservations, stock movements, and cash sessions.
11. The internal receipt shows the confirmed sale and is clearly non-fiscal.
12. Strict TDD and observed verification evidence are recorded for implemented behavior; unavailable checks remain explicitly pending.
